This same problem occurs with data.

  • Provide too much detail and the patterns within the data are not clear.
  • Zoom out too much and now things are so general that it’s impossible to find anything specific.

The goal of an analyst is to find the right balance of detail / scope.

What is a Summary Statistic?

A summary statistic is any quantity that describes a dataset.

You know many of these: mean (average), median, mode, standard deviation.

And there are plenty of others too: skewness, kurtosis, interquartile mean….

Most of these generalize down into 2 categories

  • Measures of location
  • Measures of spread

Let me explain: If you were looking at data on _____ and saw that the average (mean) was ______, that would tell you the location of _______. Similarly, the median would be a measure of location. It has a slightly different definition and features (for example, it is more resistant to outliers), but it is also a way for us to describe where on the number line this generally takes place.

On the other hand, we may see that our values vary a ton. This is where spread comes into play. For example, we say our standard deviation is _____, which describes how much we expect our value to vary from its location (ie. the mean).

Put this all together, and you have the makings of a basic probability distribution!
